The combined dataset contains 1269 observations, with each row
representing a dumpster collected by Mr. Trash Wheel, Professor Trash
Wheel, or Gwynnda. Key variables include the collection date, trash
weight in tons, volume in cubic yards, and counts of items such as
plastic bottles and cigarette butts. I cleaned the column names, removed
rows without dumpster numbers, standardized year and month values, and
rounded Mr. Trash Wheel’s sports ball counts to integers. I added a
variable identifying each wheel before combining the datasets. Variables
not recorded for a particular wheel are represented by missing values.
Professor Trash Wheel collected a total of 294.96 tons of trash, and
Gwynnda collected 18,120 cigarette butts in June 2022.
