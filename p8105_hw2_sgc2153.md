The baseline file contains 483 recruited participants. I skipped the
explanatory first row, cleaned the column names, and treated “.” as a
missing value for MCI onset age. I encoded sex and APOE4 carrier status
as factors with descriptive labels. Four participants had MCI onset at
or before baseline and were excluded, leaving 479 eligible participants.
The cleaned dataset has one row per participant and includes baseline
age, sex, years of education, APOE4 status, and MCI onset age. Among the
eligible participants, 93 developed MCI during follow-up. The average
baseline age was 65.03 years, and 30% of women were APOE4 carriers.

resulting dataset contains 471 participants, with five scheduled time
