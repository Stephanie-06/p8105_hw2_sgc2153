exits, including the line, station name, station latitude and longitude,
routes served, whether entry is allowed, vending availability, entrance
type, and ADA compliance. I cleaned the column names, retained the
required variables, and converted entry from “YES”/“NO” to TRUE/FALSE.
The resulting dataset has 1868 rows and 19 columns. These data are not
yet fully tidy because routes served are spread across eleven columns
rather than stored in a single route variable.
